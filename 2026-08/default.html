📢 President's Message

Hello MARC Family!

It's August in Texas, and things are heating up faster than the debate over whether beans belong in Texas chili!

Speaking of things heating up, last month our own Tom Kihm (KJ6PST) presented an outstanding discussion on Balun Basics at McKinney Fire Station #7. Tom shared a wealth of knowledge on the subject, and the lively questions from attendees made for an engaging and informative morning. A big thank you to Tom for taking the time to share his expertise with the club. If you missed the presentation, or would like to watch it again, you can find the video on the club's YouTube channel. We're already looking forward to whatever discussion or class Tom has in store for MARC next!

With school starting back soon, many families are squeezing in one last summer adventure. Whether you're chasing DX from a park, activating a POTA site, or simply trying to keep up with the family at the beach, remember to take care of yourself. Wear sunscreen, find some shade when you can, and drink plenty of water. Think of it like Field Day at Erwin Park, except this time, staying hydrated is worth even more than bonus points!

Speaking of family, I've always believed that MARC is much more than just an amateur radio club, it's an extended family. Time and again, our members have stepped up to support one another during life's challenges. Our Discord server includes a Health & Welfare channel where members can share prayer requests, ask for encouragement, or simply let others know when they could use a helping hand. I've personally used the channel during some recent health concerns, and I'm truly grateful for the prayers and support I received. If you ever have a need, please don't hesitate to reach out. That's what family is for.

Don't forget that every Saturday morning a group of us gathers for breakfast at IHOP on the northeast corner of US-75 and Highway 380. Most of us arrive around 7:00 AM (or a little earlier) and have our orders in by 7:30. If you haven't joined us yet, we'd love to have you. It's always a great morning filled with good food, plenty of laughs, and even better fellowship.

Our next general club meeting will be Tuesday, August 11, at 7:00 PM at Spring Creek BBQ in McKinney. Come a little early for our popular show-and-tell session, grab a plate of great barbecue, and enjoy an evening of learning, fellowship, and amateur radio. Whether you're a longtime member or attending for the first time, you'll find a welcoming group that's always happy to see new faces.

Until then, stay cool, stay safe, and we'll look forward to catching you on the air, at breakfast, or at our next club meeting!

73,
- Robert Keister, WZ5V

🖥️ HamStation HQ - Free Lifetime License for MARC Members

Ken Kalous (KE5VXX) introduced his new project on the weekly repeater net and reached out to share it with the club.

HamStation is an all-in-one amateur radio application for Windows, Linux, and macOS, designed to bring many of the tools we use every day into a single, modern program. It includes logging, satellite tracking with Doppler correction, interactive maps, solar and propagation information, APRS support, equipment inventory, QSL card design, Morse code practice, and much more.

Ken is actively developing HamStation and adding new features based on feedback from the amateur radio community. The app is fully functional and free forever; an optional paid upgrade removes the nag screens.

Best of all, Ken is offering a free lifetime license to everyone at MARC. To learn more, follow the project, or try future beta releases, visit HamStationHQ.com.

- Ken Kalous, KE5VXX

🌍 Join Us!

The McKinney Amateur Radio Club (MARC) welcomes everyone, whether you're just discovering the world of Amateur Radio or you're an experienced operator looking to connect with fellow enthusiasts. Our club is open to anyone with a passion for radio communication, a curiosity for learning, or a desire to expand their skills. Amateur Radio is a fascinating, educational, and rewarding hobby, and we invite you to explore everything MARC has to offer.

As a MARC member, you’ll have access to a variety of engaging activities and resources, including regular meetings, special events, and hands-on community service projects. These events allow members to enhance their radio skills, contribute to emergency preparedness initiatives, and build meaningful connections within the amateur radio community. Joining MARC is a great way to grow personally while making a positive impact.

Interested in becoming a member? We'd love to have you! Feel free to reach out to our club secretary at secretary@mckinneyarc.org for more details. You can also visit our website at mckinneyarc.org to access a membership application and learn more about the benefits of joining, such as access to club resources, participation in exclusive events, and opportunities to collaborate with fellow operators.

If you're not ready to join just yet, why not attend one of our monthly meetings or public events? These gatherings provide a fantastic way to meet club members, ask questions, and experience the excitement of amateur radio firsthand.

Prefer to reach us by mail? Feel free to contact us at:

Secretary, McKinney Amateur Radio Club
P.O. Box 267
McKinney, TX 75070

We look forward to hearing from you and hope to see you at an upcoming meeting or event. Join us and become part of a passionate and welcoming community dedicated to the world of Amateur Radio!

MARC E-MAIL REFLECTOR - GROUPS.IO

MARC maintains a chat group on groups.io to facilitate club communication between meetings and newsletters. Sign up for this service by visiting https://groups.io/g/McKinneyARC. Your application will be approved quickly.

The purposes of the MARC email reflector are: 1) Facilitate the timely distribution of MARC information and news to club members. 2) Allow for the sharing of amateur radio related topics among club members. 3) Provide a forum for the discussion of amateur related information among club members. 4) Act as a marketplace for the buying and selling of amateur radio and computer equipment and services. Club members should utilize good judgment with regard to topics or content that are posted on the MARC reflector. To that end it is recommended that political and religious topics or content be avoided unless directly related to amateur radio. In addition, personal attacks in any form will not be tolerated.
